Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

1. Who we are

Postador Social is a Windows desktop application developed and used by Glautium Gabriel (contact: glautiumgabriel@gmail.com), Jequié-BA, Brazil, to publish videos and photos to his own social media accounts and channels.

2. YouTube API Services

Postador Social uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube features of Postador Social you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

With your authorization the app requests two scopes: youtube.upload, to upload the video you choose to your own channel (videos.insert), and youtube.readonly, used only for one channels.list (mine=true) call to show the name of the authorized channel so you do not upload to the wrong channel. No other YouTube data is read, stored or analyzed.

3. Data the app accesses

  • YouTube: the name and ID of the authorized channel, and the upload permission described above.
  • Instagram and Facebook: account or Page ID and name, and permission to publish media.
  • TikTok: the account nickname and avatar (creator info) and permission to publish videos. A video and its caption are sent to TikTok only after the user confirms the post.
  • The video files, photos, titles and captions that the user chooses to publish.

4. How data is used

Data is used only to publish the content the user chooses, at the time the user chooses, and to show the result (published or error). Nothing is used for advertising, sale, profiling or analytics.

5. Storage on your device

The app runs only on the user's own computer. There is no Postador Social server. Access tokens and the account names above are stored only on that computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), and are never written to log files. The app uses no cookies and no tracking.

6. Sharing

We do not share data with anyone. Content goes only to the social network the user selects (Google/YouTube, Meta, TikTok), which handles it under its own policies.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Postador Social's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Deleting your data and revoking access

Removing an account in Postador Social immediately deletes its tokens and stored account name from the computer. You can also revoke access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions (security settings page); Instagram in Settings › Apps and websites; Facebook in Settings › Business integrations; TikTok in Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.

9. Contact

Questions or data requests: glautiumgabriel@gmail.com.
